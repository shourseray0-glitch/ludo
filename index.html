Ludo

How to play

  • Tap the dice to roll. Roll a 6 to bring a piece out.
  • Tap a bouncing piece to move it. If only one can move, it moves by itself.
  • Land on your opponent to send them back home. Squares with a ★ and the coloured start squares are safe.
  • A 6 or a capture gives you another roll. Get all 4 pieces to the middle to win. You need the exact number to finish.
